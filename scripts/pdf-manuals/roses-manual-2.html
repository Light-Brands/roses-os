Phase B  ·  Preparing the Space

Preparing Your Physical Space

Before meditation, prepare your physical environment to support the energetic work. The external space should mirror the internal intention: clean, clear, quiet, and intentionally held.

Protection of the Space

Now prepare the room you're in so that it's clean and protected:

  • Create 4 Golden Roses each with a golden stem and grounded.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the top four corners of the room, joining in the center of the ceiling.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the four lower corners, joining in the center of the floor.
  • Create a golden line from the upper point to the lower point, unifying the energy.
  • From the center of the floor, where the 4 lines meet, create a transparent grounding cord to the center of the Earth and expand it to the edges of the room.
  • See the whole room vibrating in gold.
3
Cleansing the Space

Cleansing of the Space

With protection established, the cleansing follows. A Golden Rose is set above the grid and passed downward through its entire structure, removing any foreign, stagnant, or disruptive energies inside the room. This Rose comes down to be transmuted in the center of the earth through the Grounding Cord of the room.

Energetic Authority

Owning Your Space

Own the room to have authority over the space where you are meditating:

  • Create 4 golden lines from the crown chakra to the top 4 corners of the room.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the root chakra to the four bottom corners of the room.
  • If meditating in an open-air space, create a dome or golden room around you and do all the techniques as if indoors.
Important Remarks

If you are meditating in a space that is not under your energetic authority, limit your protection to the immediate area around you, then explode the four Golden Roses outward upon completion. If the space is yours — or falls within your energetic authority — the four Golden Roses may remain, continuing to hold and protect it.

Phase C  ·  Chakra Cleansing

Cleansing of Each Chakra

Each individual chakra is cleansed using roses. The roses address two dimensions: Dynamics of the Past — energetic patterns, imprints, and blockages carried from past experiences, and Dynamics of the Present — current energetic influences, relationships, and situations affecting the chakra.

  • On the outside of the Aura, create two grounded roses (in the chakra's color), one behind you and one in front, at the level of the chakra. The front Rose cleanses energies from the present; the back Rose cleanses energies from the past.
  • Create and explode several Roses to cleanse the chakra deeply.
  • Create an unrooted rose (in the chakra's color) to cleanse the corresponding Aura layer at the height of the chakra.
  • Create and explode several Roses to cleanse the Aura layers.
  • Create a grounded rose at the chakra level outside the Aura to absorb all your dispersed energy in that vibration. Explode it — your energy returns elevated and purified.

This procedure should be done equally for all chakras, starting from the first and ascending in order up to the seventh.

Aura Layer Cleansing

Cleansing of Each Aura Layer

The aura is composed of seven layers, each corresponding to a chakra. In Level 2, each layer is individually cleansed from the 1st to the 7th layer.

The Seven Aura Layers
  1. 1st Aura layer — Root
  2. 2nd Aura layer — Sacral
  3. 3rd Aura layer — Solar Plexus
  4. 4th Aura layer — Heart
  5. 5th Aura layer — Throat
  6. 6th Aura layer — Third Eye
  7. 7th Aura layer — Crown

Cleanse each aura layer using an unrooted rose in the corresponding chakra color. Create and explode several roses for each layer, working from the 1st layer up to the 7th.

11
Recovery

Energy Recovery Rose in Depth

Once the cleansing of the chakra and its corresponding aura layer is complete, the energy recovery process can unfold at their vibration, if you feel called to do so. A recovery Rose may be created attuned to that specific frequency, drawing your energy back and restoring it at that very level. You can do it from the 1st to the 7th chakra.

Deep Cleansing

Golden Sticky Roses

Four Golden Sticky Roses are used for deep energetic cleansing of the Aura, drawing out any foreign energies that still remain after the previous clearing.

First Rose — Crown to Root

The first enters through the crown chakra, cleansing each chakra in succession down to the root.

Second Rose — Arms

The second descends to the throat chakra, where it splits into two Roses that travel the channels of the arms, exiting through the hand chakras.

13
Deep Cleansing

Golden Sticky Roses (continued)

Third Rose — Legs

The third descends to the root chakra, where it becomes two Roses that follow the channels of the legs, exiting through the foot chakras.

Fourth Rose — Full Aura

The fourth spans the full width of the Aura, descending through the entire energetic field.

Upon completion, all four either explode outside the Aura or exit through the grounding cord, transmuting the collected energies away with them.
