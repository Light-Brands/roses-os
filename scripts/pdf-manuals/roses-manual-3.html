Foundation

The 5 Bodies & 5 Levels of Existence

Everything that manifests itself in matter has an origin on a spiritual level. Everything is also energy. The denser the energy, the more it moves towards manifestation.

1. Spiritual

On the spiritual level, everything is perfect and complete. There is no suffering or adversity. It is the field of learning, where the existence of each event, relationship or being is determined. There is no value judgment — no right or wrong, good or bad. It simply is.

2. Energetic

The field of the subtle, of potential, of vibration. It's where our Aura is formed and where we work in Rose Meditation. This is where everything that will one day manifest in matter can already be felt. It's where non-incarnate energies act and where we form bonds that connect us beyond time and space.

3

3. Mental

When subtle energies densify, the first place we perceive them is in the world of ideas, thoughts, programs and beliefs. Here we confront the reality perceived by the senses with the programs that — within us — dictate how the world should be.

4. Emotional

This is where everything we feel stays. We have an emotional body where the marks of feelings that have passed through us, but which we haven't been able to release, remain. Here is the burden left by the traumas we have experienced. The emotional body connects with the physical body mainly through the organs.

5. Physical

Everything we experience in matter, with our body. The world we perceive through our five senses. The physical plane is the only one perceived objectively by everyone — that's why it's the reference where we can investigate what's happening on all other planes.

Everything that manifests itself in matter has an origin on a spiritual level. This is the same as saying that everything has a reason for being.

Spiritual Level

Breaking Spiritual Agreements

Any relationship can only exist in matter if there has been a spiritual agreement to support it. These agreements can only be created with the consent of both parts on the spiritual level and can be broken by either part through their conscious will. Each time a spiritual agreement is formed, an agreement stick is created.

To Break These Agreements
  • Create a high-vibration situation, for example right after the Roses Meditation.
  • View the agreement stick that contains all the agreements you want to break.
  • Break the stick into three pieces.
  • Create a grounded Rose.
  • Place the three pieces of stick in the Rose.
  • Explode the Rose out of the Aura.

This should always be done with great awareness, as it has very real consequences for our lives.

Energetic Level

Cutting Energetic Cords

We have energetic interactions with people all the time — when we see them, hear them, touch them and even think about them. When we form bonds, our auras connect through cords where energy passes through. When relationships end, we can find ourselves very affected by the energies that reach us through these bonds.

  • Close your eyes.
  • Visualize your Aura with cords coming out of your chakras.
  • Place your dominant hand on the 7th cervical vertebra (junction of neck and back, at the level of the 5th chakra).
  • Intend that your hand has the power to cut all the cords.
  • Make the movement of cutting the cords down to under your 1st chakra.

We can also cut cords when the relationship hasn't ended, but we feel we need more neutrality. These bonds form again very quickly when we reconnect with the person.

Applied Technique

Post-Intimacy Energy Cleansing

After sexual intercourse, to recover your energy and return the other person's energy:

  • Do the preparation techniques (grounding cord, Golden Sun, Earth & Cosmos energy, sacred space).
  • Create an orange Rose and place the person's energy in it — visualize the person, the situation, emotions. Explode. Repeat as needed.
  • Create an unrooted orange Rose to cleanse any remaining energy from your Aura layers. Explode.
  • Create a grounded orange Rose to recover all your energy left with the person. Explode.
  • If you want energetic separation, cut the cords created during intimacy.
  • Create an unrooted pink Rose, place the person on it. Visualize: "Happy, healthy, complete, body, mind, Spirit." Raise the Rose to the center of the Universe.
  • Renew grounding cord, fill with Golden Sun, discharge excess energy.

You can also use this technique for longer relationships or when ending a relationship. In this case, consider breaking the agreement sticks as well.

Professional Practice

Classes & Consultations

When you are working in any field — or when you are a teacher or lecturer — you can use Rose Meditation to maintain your energy integrity.

Before

Do all the preparation techniques (grounding cord, Golden Sun, Earth & Cosmos energy, sacred space) and cleanse and protect the physical space where the service or class will take place.

After

After saying goodbye, take a few moments to reorganize the energies and do the energy separation techniques, because during a class or service many emotional, energetic and spiritual bonds can be formed.

After a Class or Consultation

Post-Session Cleansing Steps

  • Cut the grounding cord and create a new one.
  • Fill yourself with the light of the Golden Sun.
  • Cut the cords that bind your Aura to the other person's Aura.
  • Create a grounded Rose, call your own energy into it, and explode — this recovers your energy that may have remained in the person(s)' Aura.
  • Explode the Roses of separation, protection and observation and create new ones.
  • Create a Rose, place the person/students inside and explode — this returns energies that may have remained in your Aura.
  • When closing contracts (no further plans to serve the person): break spiritual agreements.
  • Create an unrooted pink Rose, place the person(s) on it. Visualize: "Happy, healthy, complete, body, mind, Spirit." Raise the Rose to the Universe.
  • Do the Golden Sticky Rose cleansing (or full meditation if necessary).
  • New grounding cord, Golden Sun, discharge excess energy.
  • Explode the 4 roses in the corners of the room.

Inner Architecture

The Analyzer

The Analyzer is located inside the head. Its functions are to analyze, judge and criticize. It stores our programs and beliefs. Cleansing the Analyzer allows you to free yourself from limitations and perceive life more objectively.

Cleansing Procedure (repeat for each chakra color)
  • See the Analyzer in the chakra color (start with red).
  • Ground the Analyzer to the center of the Earth with a cord in that color.
  • Create a Rose (same color) grounded behind you outside your Aura at the height of the Analyzer.
  • The Rose cleanses the Analyzer in this vibration. Explode outside the Aura.
  • Repeat for all chakra colors: red, orange, yellow, green, pink, sky blue, indigo, violet.
  • See the Analyzer in gold. Create a Golden Sticky Rose that spirals inside, cleansing hidden energies. It exits through the grounding cord.
  • Close the Analyzer completely.

When closing the Analyzer you may feel your intuition working more strongly. If you need rational faculties, simply intend it and the Analyzer will open again.

Inner Architecture

Transmedium Channels

The Transmedium Channels are located at the back of the neck. It is through them that our Spirit incarnates in our body and through them that disincarnate energies can exert influence on us. Cleansing and closing them regularly increases energetic authority.

Cleansing Procedure (repeat for each chakra color)
  • See the Transmedium Channels in the chakra color (start with red).
  • Ground each channel to the center of the Earth with a cord in that color.
  • Create two Roses (same color) grounded behind you outside the Aura at the height of the channels.
  • The Roses cleanse the channels. Explode outside the Aura.
  • Repeat for all chakra colors.
  • See the channels in gold. Create two Golden Sticky Roses that spiral inside, cleansing hidden energies. They exit through the grounding cords.
  • Close the Transmedium Channels completely, 100%.

Manifestation

Mock-up

A technique for manifesting things or situations on the physical plane. Must be done 7 days in a row, for one goal at a time. Best done right after Rose Meditation when vibration is highest.

  • Create a grounded Rose and imagine what you want inside it, with as much detail as you can.
  • Create a second grounded Rose to the right — intend it to clear any energy or blockage interfering with your goal. Don't focus on what's being cleansed.
  • Create a third pink grounded Rose (Divine Love). Bring the image of something you love deeply — raise your vibration to the frequency of Love.
  • Imagine pink Divine Love energy from your heart enveloping the first Rose in a pink bubble.
  • Cut the roots of the first Rose and let it rise to the center of the Universe. Ask for it to come true if it is for the Supreme Good.
  • Explode the third Rose (pink), then the second Rose (cleansing).

Repeat for 7 consecutive days. If you miss a day, start over. Once complete, live in alignment on all levels, surrendering fulfillment to the Supreme Being.
