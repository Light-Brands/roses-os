Roses represent the spirit. They absorb all the energies, emotions and situations that don't belong to you or that no longer serve your present moment. Phase A  ·  Energy Preparation
Preparation

Getting Ready to Start

  1. Sit down and keep your spine aligned in an upright posture. Place both feet on the floor, with your hands on your knees and palms facing upwards.
  2. Concentrate, close your eyes, bring your presence into the present moment and breathe deeply and slowly.
  3. Use the power of your imagination (image in action) to visualize the techniques.

Rose Meditation is a tool for cleansing the aura and the seven main chakras, providing protection and energy balance. Through this technique it is possible to release blocked energies, energies that are not ours, become aware of our life force and deprogram patterns that prevent direct contact with our essence.

1

Grounding Cord

Create a taut grounding cord that runs from your first chakra to the center of the Earth.

The cord eliminates distractions, thoughts and blockages that prevent you from being in the present moment. It brings security, stability and helps to clear energies that are not of your essence.

With time and practice, the cord naturally becomes transparent.

2

Golden Sun

Create a Golden Sun above your head and fill the entire space of your Aura and physical body with the golden light that comes from the Sun. This sun represents the Supreme Being, it raises your vibration and fills the empty energetic spaces with Divine energy after any subtle release of energy.

Fill yourself with the light of the Golden Sun whenever you renew the Meditation, before you finish and at any time of the day when you want to raise your vibration.

3

Limits of the Aura

The aura is an energy field that surrounds our physical body. It can expand and contract. For Rose Meditation, establish your Aura 50cm around your entire body.

4

Expansion of the Grounding Cord

Expand the cord sideways to the width of the Aura, grounding the entire Aura.

5

Renewal of the Grounding Cord

Using your imagination, create a cutting tool of your choice to cut the grounding cord whenever you need to. This tool will always be the same and will become more powerful every time you use it.

  • Cut the old cord.
  • Create a new cord.
  • Expand the cord sideways to the width of the Aura every time you create a new one.

6

Four Roses of Protection, Separation & Observation

Create four Roses of the color of your choice or the color that comes to you (as long as they are high-vibration, each one can have a color) grounded on the outside of your Aura — in front, behind, to the left and to the right. Each Rose fulfills three functions:

Protection

On a daily basis, we interact with energies that are not ours. By protecting ourselves, we increase our understanding of the dynamics we experience.

Separation

It helps our Aura remain separate from other people's Aura, bringing a clearer perception of our individuality.

Observation

When we observe from a neutral state, as a spectator, we develop compassion, not judgment.

Circuit of the Energies of the Earth & the Cosmos

Energy of the Earth

We call on the energy of the Earth to bring the force of transmutation and vitality. It works like an engine that increases the power of everything we do during meditation.

See a copper-golden energy from the center of the Earth entering through the feet, up through the legs, through the coccyx and down the grounding cord. 10% rises to mix with cosmic energy.

Energy of the Cosmos

The energy of the Cosmos is pure consciousness. It helps us understand who we are, why we experience challenges, and what adjustments we can make.

See a luminous golden energy entering through the crown chakra, down the spine, through the coccyx and up the front. At the throat it splits — one part exits through the crown, two through the hands like a fountain.

Both energies continue to circulate in constant flow. This activation raises our vibration, promotes unblocking, brings up issues to resolve and provides spiritual information.

Roses to Cleanse & Recover Energy

Cleansing Rose

Create a Rose in a high-vibration color, grounded in front of you, outside your Aura. Place any uncomfortable situation on it — thoughts, people, events, fears. Intend for the Rose to suck in energy like a vacuum. Explode the Rose to cleanse and raise the vibration. Create new Roses as needed.

Recovery Rose

Create another Rose, grounded outside your Aura, and call your energy back. The Rose attracts your energy scattered throughout the universe like a magnet. Explode the Rose and receive your high vibrational and purified energy back to you.

You can create and explode as many roses as you feel like. Do the same in positive situations where you want to raise the vibration even more.

Pink Rose

After cleansing another person's energy from our Aura, we can offer them a gift:

  • Create an unrooted pink Rose.
  • Place the person in this Rose visualizing them happy, healthy, complete in body, mind and spirit.
  • Repeat in your head: "Happy, healthy, complete, body, mind, spirit."
  • While repeating, make the Rose rise towards the center of the Universe, wishing it all the best.
To End the Meditation
  • Cut the grounding cord and create a new one.
  • Fill yourself with the golden sun.
  • Place your hands on the floor to release excess energy.

Continuing the Journey

Level 2

Deeper Practice

Now you are ready to go deeper — to establish your sacred space, cleanse and protect the environments around you, and work directly with the energy of your chakras.

Sacred Space

Center of the Head

Place yourself in the center of your head, in your sacred space, where you are safe and aware of your energetic authority. It is from there that you can do the Rose Meditation or any energy work safely, clearly, clairvoyantly and saving your energy.

This space is dedicated to your Spirit, which is the purest part of you. It should always be tightly closed and protected.

Go to this place whenever you do the Rose Meditation or whenever you need to make important decisions and want to be in alignment with your spiritual essence.

Phase B  ·  Preparing the Space
10

Preparing the Room

Now prepare the room you're in so that it's clean and protected:

  • Create 4 Golden Roses each with a golden stem and a golden grounding cord.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the top four corners of the room, joining in the center of the ceiling.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the four lower corners, joining in the center of the floor.
  • Create a golden line from the upper point to the lower point, unifying the energy.
  • From the lower point, create a transparent grounding cord to the center of the Earth and expand it to the edges of the room.
  • See the whole room vibrating in gold.
  • Create a large golden Rose (not sticky) that descends from the Universe, cleanses the space and exits through the grounding cord.

Energetic Authority

Owning Your Space

Own the room to have authority over the space where you are meditating:

  • Create 4 golden lines from the crown chakra to the top 4 corners of the room.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the root chakra to the four bottom corners of the room.
  • If meditating in an open-air space, create a dome or golden room around you and do all the techniques as if indoors.
Important Remarks
  • Use these techniques for your home, workspaces, anywhere you sleep or meditate.
  • Don't use these techniques for public spaces (restaurants, airports, etc.). In public, create a golden dome around you instead.
  • You can apply these techniques to a single room, your whole house, or entire property.

Phase C  ·  Chakra Cleansing

Cleansing the Chakras

Start cleansing the chakras by creating and exploding roses. Roses work like a vacuum cleaner or magnet and suck in energies that don't belong to you:

  • On the outside of the Aura, create two grounded roses (in the chakra's color), one behind you and one in front, at the level of the chakra. The front Rose cleanses energies from the present; the back Rose cleanses energies from the past.
  • Create and explode several Roses to cleanse the chakra deeply.
  • Create an unrooted rose (in the chakra's color) to cleanse the corresponding Aura layer at the height of the chakra.
  • Create and explode several Roses to cleanse the Aura layers.
  • Create a grounded rose at the chakra level outside the Aura to absorb all your dispersed energy in that vibration. Explode it — your energy returns elevated and purified.

This procedure should be done equally for all chakras, starting from the first and ascending in order up to the seventh.

Deep Cleansing

Golden Sticky Roses

Create four golden sticky Roses. One at a time, they enter through the crown chakra, cleansing and traveling through our body:

First Rose

Enters through the crown chakra, cleansing all chakras down to the root, and exits through the grounding cord.

Second Rose

Enters through the crown, descends to the throat chakra, splits into two Roses that follow the arm channels and exit through the hand chakras.

Third Rose

Enters through the crown, descends to the root chakra, splits into two Roses that follow the leg channels and exit through the foot chakras.

Fourth Rose

Spans the entire width of the Aura, enters and descends, cleansing the entire Aura, and exits through the grounding cord.
