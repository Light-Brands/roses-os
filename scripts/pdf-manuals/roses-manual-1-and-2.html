Preparing the Room

Preparing the Room

Cleansing the Space

Now prepare the room you're in so that it's clean and protected:

  • Create 4 Golden Roses each with a golden stem and a golden grounding cord.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the top four corners of the room, joining in the center of the ceiling.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the four lower corners, joining in the center of the floor.
  • Create a golden line from the upper point to the lower point, unifying the energy.
  • From the lower point, create a transparent grounding cord to the center of the Earth and expand it to the edges of the room.
  • See the whole room vibrating in gold.
  • Create a large golden Rose (not sticky) that descends from the Universe, cleanses the space and exits through the grounding cord.
3
Energetic Authority

Owning Your Space

Own the room to have authority over the space where you are meditating:

  • Create 4 golden lines from the crown chakra to the top 4 corners of the room.
  • Create 4 golden lines from the root chakra to the four bottom corners of the room.
  • If meditating in an open-air space, create a dome or golden room around you and do all the techniques as if indoors.
Important Remarks
  • Use these techniques for your home, workspaces, anywhere you sleep or meditate.
  • Don't use these techniques for public spaces (restaurants, airports, etc.). In public, create a golden dome around you instead.
  • You can apply these techniques to a single room, your whole house, or entire property.

Phase C  ·  Chakra Cleansing

Cleansing the Chakras

Start cleansing the chakras by creating and exploding roses. Roses work like a vacuum cleaner or magnet and suck in energies that don't belong to you:

  • On the outside of the Aura, create two grounded roses (in the chakra's color), one behind you and one in front, at the level of the chakra. The front Rose cleanses energies from the present; the back Rose cleanses energies from the past.
  • Create and explode several Roses to cleanse the chakra deeply.
  • Create an unrooted rose (in the chakra's color) to cleanse the corresponding Aura layer at the height of the chakra.
  • Create and explode several Roses to cleanse the Aura layers.
  • Create a grounded rose at the chakra level outside the Aura to absorb all your dispersed energy in that vibration. Explode it — your energy returns elevated and purified.

This procedure should be done equally for all chakras, starting from the first and ascending in order up to the seventh.
