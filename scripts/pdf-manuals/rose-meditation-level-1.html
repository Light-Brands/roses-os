Rose Meditation  ·  Level 1
Preparation

Getting Ready to Start

  1. Sit down and keep your spine aligned in an upright posture. Place both feet on the floor, with your hands on your knees and palms facing upwards.
  2. Concentrate, close your eyes, bring your presence into the present moment and breathe deeply and slowly.
  3. Use the power of your imagination (image in action) to visualize the techniques.

Rose Meditation is a tool for cleansing the aura and the seven main chakras, providing protection and energy balance. Through this technique it is possible to release blocked energies, energies that are not ours, become aware of our life force and deprogram patterns that prevent direct contact with our essence.

3
1

Grounding Cord

Create a taut grounding cord that runs from your first chakra to the center of the Earth.

The cord eliminates distractions, thoughts and blockages that prevent you from being in the present moment. It brings security, stability and helps to clear energies that are not of your essence.

With time and practice, the cord naturally becomes transparent.

4
2

Golden Sun

Create a Golden Sun above your head and fill the entire space of your Aura and physical body with the golden light that comes from the Sun. This sun represents the Supreme Being, it raises your vibration and fills the empty energetic spaces with Divine energy after any subtle release of energy.

Fill yourself with the light of the Golden Sun whenever you renew the Meditation, before you finish and at any time of the day when you want to raise your vibration.

5
3

Limits of the Aura

The aura is an energy field that surrounds our physical body. It can expand and contract. For Rose Meditation, establish your Aura 20 inches (50 cm) around your entire body.

4

Expansion of the Grounding Cord

Expand the cord sideways to the width of the Aura, grounding the entire Aura.

5

Renewal of the Grounding Cord

Using your imagination, and your cutting tool, cut the grounding cord whenever you need to. This tool will always be the same and will become more powerful every time you use it.

  • Cut the old cord.
  • Create a new cord.
  • Expand the cord sideways to the width of the Aura every time you create a new one.

6

Four Roses of Protection, Separation & Observation

Create four Roses of the color of your choice or the color that comes to you (as long as they are high-vibration) grounded on the outside of your Aura — in front, behind, to the left and to the right. Each Rose fulfills three functions:

Protection

On a daily basis, we interact with energies that are not ours. By protecting ourselves, we increase our understanding of the dynamics we experience.

Separation

It helps our Aura remain separate from other people's Aura, bringing a clearer perception of our individuality.

Observation

When we observe from a neutral state, as a spectator, we develop compassion, not judgment.

7

Circuit of the Energies of the Earth & the Cosmos

Energy of the Earth

We call on the energy of the Earth to bring the force of transmutation and vitality. It works like an engine that increases the power of everything we do during meditation.

See a copper-golden energy from the center of the Earth entering through the feet, up through the legs, through the coccyx and down the grounding cord. 10% rises to mix with cosmic energy.

Energy of the Cosmos

The energy of the Cosmos is pure consciousness. It helps us understand who we are, why we experience challenges, and what adjustments we can make.

See a luminous golden energy entering through the crown chakra, down the spine, through the coccyx and up the front of the body. At the throat chakra it splits — one part exits through the crown, two through the hands like a fountain.

Both energies continue to circulate in constant flow. This activation raises our vibration, promotes unblocking, brings up issues to resolve and provides spiritual information.

8

Roses to Cleanse & Recover Energy

Cleansing Rose

Create a Rose in a high-vibration color, grounded in front of you, outside your Aura. Place any uncomfortable situation on it — thoughts, people, events, fears. Intend for the Rose to suck in energy like a vacuum.

Explode the Rose out of your Aura to cleanse the energy and raise the vibration. Create new Roses as many times as required.

Recovery Rose

Create another Rose, grounded outside your Aura, and call your energy back. The Rose attracts your energy scattered throughout the universe like a magnet.

Explode the Rose out of your Aura, and receive your high vibrational and purified energy back to you.

You can create and explode as many roses as you feel like. Do the same in positive situations where you want to raise the vibration even more.

9

Pink Rose

We can offer this rose as a gift, after cleansing another person's energy from our Aura, or mainly for ourselves:

  • Create an unrooted pink Rose.
  • Place the person or yourself in this Rose visualizing "Happy, Healthy, Whole, Body, Mind & Soul."
  • Repeat in your head: "Happy, Healthy, Whole, Body, Mind & Soul."
  • While repeating, make the Rose rise towards the center of the Universe, wishing you or the person in it all the best.

Completion

To End the Meditation

  • Cut the grounding cord and create a new one.
  • Fill yourself with the golden sun.
  • If you feel, renew any technique you feel like — the 4 roses of separation and observation; the circuit of the cosmos and earth.
  • Place your hands on the floor to release excess energy.

At any time of the day, or whenever we meditate, we blow up the four Roses and create new ones. They help us maintain neutrality.
